01 / About

A developer who also teaches

I've spent the last 10+ years at the intersection of building and teaching. By day I ship web applications — React front-ends, AWS-backed services, and the database layers that hold them together.

In parallel, I lecture computer science at PSBU University in Phnom Penh, helping the next generation of Cambodian developers find their footing.

My path started in game development, moved through agency web work at Marinax and Suka Digital, and now lives at ApexDreamLab, where I'm focused on modern, performant, beautifully made digital products.

I hold an MSIT from Norton University and dual bachelor's degrees in Computer Science and English Literature — a combination that's quietly informed everything from how I write code comments to how I run a classroom.
